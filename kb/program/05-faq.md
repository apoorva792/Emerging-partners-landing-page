**Do I need AI engineers to do this?**
No. You need engineers. The platform layer — orchestration, guardrails, observability, deployment — is already built, and Lyzr's Applied AI team builds alongside your team on the first engagements. The four-week onboarding programme and Lyzr University exist to get your existing people to delivery standard.

**Who owns the client relationship?**
You do. Lyzr sits behind your delivery. [confirm — contractual specifics are the partner team's conversation]

**My client already has Copilot / has bought OpenAI credits. Why would they need this?**
Those are assistants. This is workflow — agents that do a defined piece of operational work, inside their systems, with an audit trail. Enterprises pick Lyzr over Palantir AIP, Microsoft Copilot and Salesforce Agentforce, then sign multi-year contracts.

**How long until I can sell something?**

# What a qualified emerging partner gets

A **partner activation package**, proposed per partner. Three parts:

| | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Platform credits** | Lyzr Agent Studio and Agent Architect credits to build, test and demo with — enough to get a first client solution working before anyone writes a cheque. |
| **Joint go-to-market** | Co-branded campaigns, content, webinars, events and lead-generation assets you can put your own name on. |
| **Sales support** | Dedicated Lyzr SDR support to help you reach and engage the customer accounts you bring. |

**No figure is ever quoted.** The package has no published dollar value, and neither the
agent nor the partner team puts one on it in writing. The value is the platform, the people
and the go-to-market support — described by what they are, never priced.

**How the agent must present this**

- Always as **proposed, and subject to approval** — never as "you've been awarded" or "you qualify for".
- Only *after* the visitor has received their recommendations and picked one. Never as the opening hook.
- Keep it at this level of detail. **Never a number** — no package value, no credit amount, no SDR hours, no campaign budget. Those are the partner manager's conversation, not the agent's.
- If asked for more detail than this page holds: *"That's exactly what the partner team will walk you through — I don't want to guess at the mechanics."*

There is **one level**. No tiers, no bronze/silver/gold, no larger package to upsell.

# What every Lyzr partner gets, package or not

Sourced from lyzr.ai/partners — safe to state as fact:

- **Platform** — Agent Studio, Agent Architect, and custom business agents, deployable inside the client's own environment.
- **Joint delivery** — Lyzr's Applied AI team and Reimagination Consultants build alongside the partner's team.
- **Co-sell** — pipeline into Lyzr's enterprise and sovereign customer base, warm introductions, AWS funding routes.
- **GTM kit** — co-branded case studies, campaigns, battlecards, ROI calculators.
- **Enablement** — a four-week onboarding programme, hands-on workshops, certifications, and Lyzr University (self-paced).
- **Agent catalogue** — 100+ production-tested agents available to demo from day one.
- **Directory listing** — the global partner directory on lyzr.ai/partners.

# The reference point

**goML**: $0 → **over $1M in new AI services revenue in nine months**, while expanding their delivery bench and moving upmarket into larger enterprise deals.

Use this once, as proof that the model works for a mid-market services firm. Do not promise it as a projection for the visitor.

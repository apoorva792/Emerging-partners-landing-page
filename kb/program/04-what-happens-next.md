# The end of the conversation

The visitor finishes with a **Partner Opportunity Assessment** they can read on the page, download, and have emailed to them. It is theirs whether or not anything else happens.

Then one offer, made once, without pressure:

> "The partner team reviews these. Want to walk through it with them?"

They can say no and keep the assessment. That is a fine outcome.

# What the agent can truthfully say about the next step

- A member of the Lyzr partner team reads the assessment.
- They come back on what support makes sense for that specific company.
- Onboarding for partners who go ahead is a **four-week programme** with workshops, certification and Lyzr University access.

# What it cannot say

- Who specifically will call, or when — *POC: no routing is wired yet.*
- That a meeting is booked — *POC: no calendar integration.*
- That anything has been submitted to a CRM — *POC: nothing is written to HubSpot.*

**POC scope note.** For this build, "next steps" ends at the assessment plus an optional "have the partner team contact me" capture (email already collected at step 0). Routing, CRM write-back, owner assignment and scheduling are deliberately out of scope and get designed once the journey itself is signed off.

# The profile Lyzr is looking for

A **services business** — one that sells and delivers work to other companies — that can carry an AI service line to its own customers.

## Strong yes

- Already sells to **enterprise customers**, not only SMBs
- Can name **3+ warm enterprise accounts** they would be willing to refer to Lyzr
- Has delivery capability in-house (engineers, consultants — not a pure referral shop)
- *Ideal, not a dealbreaker:* presence in **Middle East, UK, Australia or Europe**

**Company size is not a criterion.** Do not filter, score or comment on headcount,
and never ask how many people they are. A small firm with the right accounts and
real delivery capability is a strong fit.

## Weaker fit — still worth a conversation

- Enterprise accounts but relationships are dormant rather than warm
- Outside the priority geographies

## Not a fit

- Solo consultants and individual freelancers
- Pure resellers with no delivery capability
- Companies building a competing agent platform

## Account sharing

- The agent asks for up to **five** strategic accounts.
- **Three is enough to proceed.** Five makes the assessment complete.
- **Skipping is always allowed** — "I'd rather not share these yet" must be a visible option, and the visitor still gets their full assessment. Only the qualification section of the internal summary is marked incomplete.

## The verdict is internal

The agent **never tells the visitor they qualified or didn't**. Eligibility scoring goes into the internal summary for the partner team. To the visitor it is always some version of:

> "I've put this together for the partner team. They'll come back to you on what support makes sense."

Never: "you qualify", "unfortunately you don't meet", "you're approved for the package".
